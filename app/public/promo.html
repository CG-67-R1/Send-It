What you get

Rider Coach

Ask for race-craft help when nobody is in the garage. Prep a circuit, walk corners, and lock in the layout before you roll out.

Bike Setup

Day sheets, balance, gearing, and tyre-wear photos — plus an AI that talks setup. Snapshots stay on this device until you share them.

What’s on

Upcoming MotoGP, WorldSBK, and regional road-racing dates. Tap an event for the link, or add a reminder to your phone calendar.

News you control

Headlines from motorcycle sources you can reorder. Open the article on the publisher’s site. Optional Priority 1 notify for the feed you care about most.

Q&A and trivia

Ask a racing question, check an official rule clause, or play trivia from scooter rider to Track Guru.

Your garage, your phone

Profile, avatar, setups, and track notes stay private on device. No account. AI chats you send are processed to reply — not kept as server history.

Functionality list

Home and profile

  • Personalised home Bike photo, rider avatar, and shortcuts that follow how you ride — Learn (track days) or Setup (race).
  • Onboarding Favourite bike and rider, how you ride, nickname, and optional face photo in the leathers avatar.
  • Track arrival Optional on-device greeting when you are near a known circuit, with a prompt to talk to Coach.

Rider Coach

  • RR AI Coach Technique and race-craft chat. Seeded from Track Walk, imported notes, or a track-arrival draft.
  • Track Prep Circuit briefing before the day, saved as your last Learn session on Home.
  • Track Walk Corner-by-corner notes, photos, and voice (when available). Send the session to Coach.
  • Import track notes Paste notes from Messages, attach photos, pick a catalog track, and send them to Coach.

Bike Setup

  • Bike Setup AI Suspension, gearing, and tyre questions in a dedicated setup chat.
  • Bike Setup Sheet On-device day sheet for pressures, sag, clicks, and notes — with session history and share as text.
  • Bike Balance Setup Measurement-driven balance calculator with a report you can save locally.
  • Gearing Guide Front/rear sprocket helper, powerband, and a brief you can send to Bike Setup Coach.
  • Tyre Wear Analysis Guided tyre photos plus the facts a picture cannot show, then analyse with Bike Setup Coach.
  • Bike Setup Basics Interactive suspension diagram — tap a hotspot for road vs track guidance.

News, calendar, and knowledge

  • Headlines Aggregated motorcycle news (Settings → Open News). Source order, custom RSS, Priority 1 notify.
  • What’s On calendar MotoGP, WorldSBK, ASBK and more. Add reminder uses your device calendar.
  • Ask Live Q&A with source links. Hinted toward Coach or Bike Setup when that is the better tool.
  • Official rule check Look up MoMS clauses from the bundled rule knowledge — informational, not a licence substitute.
  • Trivia Quiz with fail/pass tiers (Track rider / Track Guru). Best score stays on device.
  • FAQs Searchable Coach and Bike Setup answers; tap through to the matching AI chat.

Informational only — not professional race or mechanical advice. News headlines link to publisher sites; copyright stays with those publishers.
