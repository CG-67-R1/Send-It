## Goal

Best possible **text descriptors** so a Custom GPT can interpret a **user-uploaded** tyre photo: spatial features first, texture second.

## Anti-confusion

Never map “rough / shredded / rippled” → cold tear by default. Many patterns look shredded on a phone. Classify by:

1. Front or rear  
2. Lean zone (centre / mid-shoulder / outer third)  
3. Band width, continuity, uniformity around the tyre  
4. Morphology (deep undercut vs shallow smear vs fine grain vs groove lip vs blue bloom)

## Quick signatures

| Class | Photo must-see |
|-------|----------------|
| Cold tear | Narrower deep undercut / fingernail flaps; sharp contrast band |
| Hot tear | Wider shallow smear or tear-with-smooth-face; arcs inward; edge balls ~3–5 mm |
| Suspension | Width changes around tyre **or** torn→clean→torn; groove leading/trailing lip |
| Geometry (light front) | Front; narrow ~5–10 mm continuous mid-shoulder band |
| Geometry (heavy front) | Front; outer-third wear; large band |
| Blue bloom | Iridescent oils after cool — not a tear |
| Good wear | Even fine “tide” grain; no deep flaps or melt arcs |
