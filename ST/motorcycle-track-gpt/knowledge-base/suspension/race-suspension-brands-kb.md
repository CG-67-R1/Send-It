## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

- Use brand/model data to identify what a rider has fitted and what adjusters are available.
- Spring rate selection is rider-weight dependent — see the spring rate calculator section.
- Adjuster click ranges are starting points; always note current position before changes.
- Never recommend an internal rebuild (oil weight, shim stack, valve) without confirmed make/model/year and a technician.

## ÖHLINS

**Origin**: Sweden. OEM supplier to MotoGP, WSBK, BSB. Most widely fitted aftermarket brand in ASBK.

### TTX GP Rear Shock (race-level flagship)

**Type**: Twin-tube, pressurised nitrogen, fully adjustable
**Applications**: WSBK/BSP/Supersport — available for most 600–1000cc platforms
**Key specs (general)**:
- Piston diameter: 46 mm (GP) / 36 mm (standard TTX)
- Nitrogen pressure: ~10 bar pre-charge
- Adjusters: Spring preload, high-speed compression, low-speed compression, rebound, ride height
- Typical adjuster range: Compression 0–20 clicks, Rebound 0–20 clicks (varies by application)
- Spring free length: ~150 mm (common)
- Spring rate range: ~55–120 N/mm (application-dependent; 80–95 N/mm typical 600cc Supersport)
- Ride height range: typically ±10 mm from baseline

**Setup reference (starting point for 600cc Supersport, ~75 kg rider in gear)**:
- Sag: 28–35 mm (race); 30–40 mm (track day)
- Free sag: 8–18 mm (per Öhlins manual)
- Spring rate: 80–95 N/mm common; 95–110 N/mm for heavier riders or 1000cc
- Compression: mid-range as baseline; increase 2–3 clicks for bumpy/high-speed tracks
- Rebound: mid-range baseline; slower (fewer clicks out) for smooth tracks, faster for bumpy

### NIX30 Fork Cartridge (race road-racing standard)

**Type**: 30 mm closed cartridge kit — replaces OEM internals
**Applications**: Supersport 600, Superbike 1000 (fitment-specific)
**Key specs**:
- Cartridge diameter: 30 mm
- Oil weight recommendation: 7.5W SAE (factory baseline); 5W for more compliant, heavier for stiffer
- Oil level: ~160 mm from top of tube (with cartridge installed); check application manual
- Combined spring rate: ~9.75 N/mm when two springs used in series (application-specific)
- Adjusters: Compression (external, typically 20–25 clicks), Rebound (external, typically 20–25 clicks)
- Spring rates: typically 0.85–1.05 N/mm (0.85–1.05 kg/mm) for 600cc; 0.95–1.20 N/mm for 1000cc

**Setup reference**:
- Sag: 30–38 mm (race); 35–42 mm (track day)
- Oil level changes: +10 mm = stiffer mid-to-end stroke, sharper feel; -10 mm = softer, more compliant
- Start compression mid-range; adjust 1–2 clicks at a time

### FGRT300 / FG900 Fork (full race replacement)

**Type**: Complete race fork leg — not a cartridge upgrade
**Applications**: BSB/WSBK OEM supply; also sold aftermarket for specific platforms
**Key specs**:
- Diameter: 43 mm (standard) or 48 mm (large bore)
- Fully adjustable: spring preload, compression (hi/lo), rebound
- TTX cartridge-style internals

## WP SUSPENSION (WP APEX PRO)

**Origin**: Austria. KTM Group brand. OEM for KTM, Husqvarna, GasGas. Used in MotoGP via Tech3 and satellite teams.

### APEX PRO 7746 Shock

**Type**: Remote reservoir, fully adjustable road-race shock
**Key specs**:
- Piston: 46 mm
- Piston rod: 18 mm
- Adjusters: Rebound, high-speed compression, low-speed compression, spring preload, ride height
- Technology: Derived from Supersport World Championship
- Nitrogen reservoir: Separate canister for consistent damping under heat
- Standard tools required (no special WP tools needed for adjustment)

### APEX PRO 8750 Shock

**Type**: Premium race shock — hydraulic preload adjuster
**Key specs**:
- Piston: 46 mm (implied from product family)
- Hydraulic adjustable preload (no manual ring spanner required)
- Optimised pressure-balanced internal design
- Rebound spring for stability under braking and acceleration
- Potentiometer mounting points available for data logging

### APEX PRO 6746 Shock

**Type**: CNC aluminium lightweight shock
**Key specs**:
- Enlarged nitrogen tank for improved cooling and consistent damping under sustained use
- CNC-milled aluminium construction
- Adjustable: rebound, compression, spring preload

### APEX PRO Fork Cartridges

**Type**: Drop-in cartridge kits for WP and conventional forks
**Key specs**:
- Available for 43 mm and 48 mm fork tubes
- Fully adjustable compression and rebound
- Specific to bike application — verify fitment before specifying
- OEM WP fork owners: APEX PRO cartridges maintain identical tube dimensions

## NITRON

**Origin**: UK. Used in BSB, international road racing, and endurance.

### NTR R3 Shock (road-racing flagship)

**Type**: Piggyback reservoir, fully adjustable, nitrogen charged
**Key specs**:
- Adjusters: 3-way — spring preload, compression (hi/lo speed separate), rebound
- Compression adjustment range: typically 15–25 clicks total
- Rebound adjustment range: typically 15–25 clicks total
- Spring rate: custom-specified at order — specify rider weight, bike, and intended use
- Ride height: adjustable via eye-end or clevis depending on application
- Service interval: annually or every 20 track days (whichever first)

### NTR R1 Shock

**Type**: Standard aftermarket shock — single compression adjuster
**Key specs**:
- Adjusters: spring preload, rebound, single compression
- Entry-level Nitron; suitable for club racing and track days
- Service interval: annually

### Fork cartridge kits

**Type**: Internal cartridge kits for conventional forks
- Full compression and rebound adjustment
- Application-specific valving; specify bike, riding style, and weight at order
- Oil weight: typically 5W–7.5W depending on temperature and application

## BITUBO

**Origin**: Italy. Used in CIV (Italian Superbike Championship) and club road racing.

### WME Series (rear shock, road racing)

**Type**: Monoshock, nitrogen pressurised, adjustable
**Key specs**:
- Adjusters: spring preload, compression, rebound
- Nitrogen reservoir: built-in or piggyback depending on model
- Application: 600–1000cc Supersport and Superbike platforms
- Ride height adjustment: available on WME-RH variants

### JBH Fork Cartridge Kit

**Type**: Closed-cartridge kit — replaces OEM internals
**Key specs**:
- Piston size: ~25 mm (track), ~30 mm (spirited), ~35 mm (street) — stiffer feel at smaller piston for track use
- Adjusters: compression and rebound (separate external adjusters)
- Oil recommendations: 5W–7.5W typically; verify with Bitubo fitment guide
- Installation: "Quick Install" system — does not require fork disassembly beyond cap removal on some models

## ANDREANI GROUP (Misano / Misano EVO)

**Origin**: Italy. Widely fitted in CIV, club racing, track days. Distribution across Europe and Australia.

### Misano EVO Cartridge Kit

**Type**: Complete internal cartridge replacement
**Key specs**:
- Full high-speed and low-speed compression adjustment
- Full rebound adjustment
- Spring rates: typically 0.80–1.00 N/mm for 600cc depending on rider weight; 0.90–1.10 N/mm for 1000cc
- Factory spring: often 0.90 kg delivered — may need to swap for rider weight
- Cap phase: critical installation step — correct cap alignment determines damping direction; verify with Andreani instructions
- Oil viscosity: 19.0 cSt at 40°C specified (approximately 7.5W equivalent)
- Service interval: 50–100 hours track use

**Note**: Spring rate 0.80 kg springs can be substituted where factory 0.90 kg feels too stiff for lighter riders (below 65 kg in gear).

## SPRING RATE SELECTION GUIDE

**General principle**: Spring rate must match rider + gear weight to achieve correct sag and prevent bottoming or excessive stiffness.

### Fork spring rate (N/mm or kg/mm — same number different unit)

| Rider weight in gear (kg) | 600cc Supersport | 1000cc Superbike |
|---------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| < 65 kg                   | 0.80–0.88         | 0.88–0.95         |
| 65–75 kg                  | 0.85–0.95         | 0.95–1.05         |
| 75–85 kg                  | 0.90–1.00         | 1.00–1.10         |
| 85–95 kg                  | 0.95–1.05         | 1.05–1.15         |
| > 95 kg                   | 1.00–1.10         | 1.10–1.20         |

### Rear spring rate (N/mm)

| Rider weight in gear (kg) | 600cc Supersport | 1000cc Superbike |
|---------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| < 65 kg                   | 70–80             | 80–90             |
| 65–75 kg                  | 80–90             | 90–100            |
| 75–85 kg                  | 88–98             | 95–110            |
| 85–95 kg                  | 95–105            | 105–118           |
| > 95 kg                   | 100–115           | 110–125           |

**Note**: These are indicative ranges. Track style (smooth vs bumpy), riding position, and chassis geometry all influence the correct rate. Start at mid-range and adjust based on sag measurement.

## SAG REFERENCE

| Measurement | Race target | Track-day target |
|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| Fork sag (rider on bike, full gear) | 30–38 mm | 35–42 mm |
| Rear sag (rider on bike, full gear) | 28–35 mm | 30–40 mm |
| Free sag (rear, rider off)          | 5–15 mm   | 5–15 mm  |

Free sag < 5 mm = spring too stiff or preload too high.
Free sag > 20 mm = spring too soft.

## OIL WEIGHT GUIDE (FORK OIL)

| Fork oil weight | Viscosity effect | Typical use |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| 2.5W / 5W       | Lighter / softer | Cold climates, very bumpy tracks, compliance priority |
| 7.5W            | Standard race baseline (most Öhlins, Andreani specs) | General track use |
| 10W             | Heavier / stiffer | Smooth tracks, high-speed circuits, firm feel preferred |
| 15W             | Very stiff | Rarely used; only for specific high-load applications |

Viscosity at 40°C: 5W ≈ 15–20 cSt; 7.5W ≈ 19–25 cSt; 10W ≈ 25–35 cSt.

## ADJUSTER DIRECTION REFERENCE

| Adjuster | Clockwise (IN) | Counter-clockwise (OUT) |
|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| Compression | Stiffer | Softer |
| Rebound | Slower | Faster |
| Preload | More preload (stiffer) | Less preload |

**Always count from fully closed (clockwise to stop) outward. Never force past stops.**

## COMMON DIAGNOSTIC LINKS

| Symptom | Check first | Then |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Front dives on brakes | Fork compression too soft, or springs too soft | Check sag, increase compression 2 clicks |
| Rear bouncing on exits | Rebound too fast | Slow rebound 2 clicks (CW) |
| Chattering on exits | Tyre temp / pressure first; then rear rebound | Check hot pressure, then slow rebound |
| Bike won't turn | Check sag, tyre pressure, entry speed first | Geometry (rake/trail) last resort |
| High-side on throttle | Rear rebound too slow | Fast rebound 1-2 clicks (CCW) |
| Front push mid-corner | Check tyre pressure first; then fork compression | Increase compression slightly |
