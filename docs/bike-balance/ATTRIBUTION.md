# Citation policy: Bike Balance Setup

## Allowed in user-facing Sources

Only:

1. Published books (with author, year, title, edition/ISBN where available)
2. Published journals
3. Publicly accessible OEM or official product documentation

Currently approved:

- Cossalter, V. (2006). Motorcycle Dynamics (2nd ed.). ISBN 978-1-4303-0861-4.
- Foale, T. (2006). Motorcycle Handling and Chassis Design: The Art and Science (2nd ed.). ISBN 978-84-933354-3-4.
- Yamaha Motor Co. public product / dealer technical specifications for YZF-R6 (2020 model year)

## Not allowed

- Unofficial guides, private notes, or personal documents
- Naming people or products that are not the authors or publishers of an approved source above
- Decorative or AI-style formatting symbols in page prose (em dashes, arrows, bullets-as-ornament, checkmark glyphs). Mathematical symbols are allowed only in equations and equation displays.

## Implementation

User-facing citation strings live in `app/src/calc/bikeBalance/citations.ts`.
