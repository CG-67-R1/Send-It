RoadRacer

RoadRacer is a pocket coach for track-day riders and club racers. You tell it how you ride, then it helps you learn the circuit, set the bike, and get a briefing that matches your level — from first track days through racing.

Get on the App Store @roadracer.au

Built around Australian circuits

It is built around Australian road-race circuits, with Coach, Bike Setup, Track Details, calendar, and Q&A in one place.

I built it because track-day and club racers need coaching and setup help on the phone.

In the app

Track Details

The picture is the track. Each number on it has a row below so you can add notes. Australian circuits — zoom the map, then tap a corner in the list.

Coach

Coach matches how you ride. RoadRacer AI uses your answers so Coach and Bike Setup stay at the right level of detail.

Bike Setup

Day sheet, Bike Balance, Gearing Guide, Tyre Wear from a photo, and Bike Setup Basics — on the phone, at your level.

Track Prep and Track Walk

Briefings before you ride. Notes you type, photograph, or speak, kept with the circuit.

Events

Calendar for MotoGP, WorldSBK, and ASBK (identification only), with add-to-calendar.

Q&A

Ask, trivia, FAQs, and an official rule check as an informational aid — not a substitute for the rule book.

How you ride

Copy and Coach respect the rider’s level: track days only, intermediate, I race, just love bikes, or local racing. A Mallala track-day rider is not spoken to like a Superbike debrief.

On the device

Your profile, avatar, setups, and track notes stay on your device. AI chats you send go to the RoadRacer API and may be processed by OpenAI; chat history is not kept on our server after the reply. RoadRacer is a free companion app in the current product (no in-app purchases on file).
