What stays on my device?

Your profile, avatar, setups and track notes stay on your device. AI chats you send go to the RoadRacer API and may be processed by OpenAI. Chat history is not kept on the RoadRacer server after the reply. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I use it for technical questions?

Q&A includes questions, trivia and FAQs. Coaching, setup and Q&A responses are informational aids. Use a qualified technician, coach or the official rule book for verification.
