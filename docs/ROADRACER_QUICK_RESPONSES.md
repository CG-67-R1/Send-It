# RoadRacer — quick responses

This version of the app is called **RoadRacer**. Never call it anything else in conversation, App Store copy, or reviews.

---

## What’s the app about?

**Short**

RoadRacer is a pocket coach for track-day riders and club racers. You tell it how you ride, then it helps you learn the circuit, set the bike, and get a briefing that matches your level — from first track days through racing.

**If they want more**

It is built around Australian road-race circuits, with Coach, Bike Setup, Track Details, calendar, and Q&A in one place.

---

## What can it do?

It does the jobs you’d otherwise split across notes, forums, and a race engineer you don’t have:

- **Coach** — race-craft and riding questions, in simple language for track days, or more detail if you race.
- **Track Details** — map of the circuit (numbers on the picture), distance, weather, ASBK records where we have them, and a row per corner for your notes.
- **Track Prep** — a briefing for the day you’re going.
- **Track Walk** — corner notes, photos, voice, then send that to Coach.
- **Bike Setup** — AI, a day sheet on the phone, balance, gearing, tyre-wear from a photo, and a basics picture of the bike.
- **Events** — MotoGP, WorldSBK, ASBK, with a reminder on your calendar.
- **Q&A** — ask a question, trivia, FAQs, and an official-rules check.

Coach and setup follow **How you ride** in Settings, so a track-day rider is not talked to like a national racer.

---

## Why did you build it?

**Short**

I built it because track-day and club racers need coaching and setup help on the phone.

**Longer**

Most of us get to the paddock with a phone full of screenshots and no one to ask. Club racing and track days don’t come with a crew chief. I wanted one app that knows the Australian circuits, talks at the rider’s level, and keeps setup and notes on the device — so you can walk the track, mark a corner, and get a sensible answer before the next session.
