## Kill the channel if

- A post calls RoadRacer a race engineer, or implies one
- A post guarantees laps, lap times, or a personal best
- A post carries an invented number (pressure, clicks, sag, sprocket pair, install count, rating)
- A post says “upload your data”
- Zero store-link taps **and** zero emails after the full 30 days

First four: stop and delete the same day. Fifth: 30-day verdict.
