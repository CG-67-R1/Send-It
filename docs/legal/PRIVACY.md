# RoadRacer Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 July 2026

RoadRacer (“we”, “the app”) is a motorcycle road-racing companion app. This policy explains what data is handled and how.

## Data stored on your device

The following are stored locally on your phone or browser (AsyncStorage / device storage). They are **not** uploaded to a RoadRacer account:

- Onboarding profile answers (favourite bike/rider, activity, nickname, avatar choice)
- Bike photo and avatar face photo (if you add them)
- Day Setup Sheet, saved setup snapshots, and Bike Balance calculator state
- Track Walk notes and photos you save
- News source preferences and notification settings
- Trivia high score

You can export or delete local data from **Profile & settings → Your data & privacy**.

## Data sent to our API / AI providers

When you use **RR AI Coach**, **RR Bike Setup**, or **Q&A Ask / Official rule check**, the text (and any attachments you choose to send) is transmitted to the RoadRacer API and may be processed by **OpenAI** to generate a reply. Chat history is not retained on our server after the response is returned.

Custom RSS feed URLs you add are fetched by our API to show headlines. We do not use those URLs for advertising.

## Location

If you enable track arrival reminders, the app uses **foreground location** while open to detect when you are near a known circuit. Location is processed on-device for that feature and is not used to build a travel history account.

## Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to set a bike photo, avatar face, or attach images to Coach. Microphone access for the camera is disabled.

## Crash reporting

If Sentry is configured for a build, crash and error diagnostics may be sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs. No coaching conversation content is intentionally sent to Sentry.

## Sharing

Features such as “Share as text” use your device’s system share sheet. Content leaves the device only when **you** pick a destination (Messages, email, etc.).

## Children

RoadRacer is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the age required by your local laws for this type of service.

## Contact

## United Kingdom / GDPR note

For users in the United Kingdom: we do not operate a RoadRacer account system. Local profile, setup, and Track Walk data stay on your device unless you export or share them. AI requests you send are processed to produce a reply and are not retained on our server as chat history after the response. You may contact us at the email above to ask privacy questions. Where UK GDPR / UK Data Protection Act rights apply to personal data we control, we will respond in line with those obligations.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
