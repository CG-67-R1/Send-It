# UK marketing pathways (post-build)

**Audience:** UK track-day riders and club racers (highest density fit for Track Walk, Coach, Calendar, Headlines).  
**Success signal:** Installs and Coach/Track Walk sessions on UK circuits — not MotoGP-weekend vanity spikes alone.

## Pathway 1 — Track-day operators

**Partners to approach**

- MSV Bike Trackdays — https://bike.msvtrackdays.com  
- No Limits Trackdays — https://www.nolimitstrackdays.com  
- 2–3 independents at Brands / Donington / Cadwell open days

**Offer**

- QR on briefing sheets / booking confirmation emails → App Store UK listing  
- “UK circuit walk + coach” 10-minute demo at Brands Hatch / Donington pitlane open days  
- Optional ACU CTC/BRA date co-marketing (licence-day captive audience)

**Assets**

- One-pager PDF (app value: walk corners, tyre read, day setup sheet)  
- QR deep-link to ASC UK storefront / TestFlight if soft launch

## Pathway 2 — Club racing ladder + BSB paddock

**Clubs / series**

- Bemsee — https://www.bemsee.net  
- Thundersport GB — https://www.thundersportgb.com  
- NG Road Racing — https://www.ngroadracing.org  
- No Limits Racing  
- BSB support-class paddock sampling (Donington / Silverstone)

**Tactics**

- Paddock flyer at club race weekends  
- Presence on ACU assessment weekends  
- Soft ask: “save your track notes between sessions”

## Pathway 3 — UK content / community

**Channels**

- MCN & Bennetts BikeSocial-aligned tips (app already scrapes both)  
- Facebook / WhatsApp UK track-day groups  
- 2–3 UK YouTube track-day creators (Brands / Cadwell / Donington corner walks)

**Content format**

- 30–60s “corner walk + tyre read” clips  
- Caption CTA: download RoadRacer → Track Walk that circuit  
- Avoid trademark abuse in paid keywords; prefer descriptive “British Superbike” / “track day” language

## Launch sequence

1. Hybrid build ships (`active.json` = au,uk) and UK GPT Knowledge uploaded  
2. Soft launch TestFlight with 20–50 UK track-day riders  
3. Operator QR pilot (one MSV weekend)  
4. Club flyer weekend  
5. Creator clip batch  

## Tracking

- Attribution: unique QR per operator / club weekend  
- In-app: Track Walk sessions by `trackId` (brands-hatch, donington, …)  
- Headlines engagement on `mcn` / `bennetts`
